Research Highlights

We study ambient intelligence and physical AI systems for mobile, wearable, IoT, and robotic platforms, with emphasis on repurposed multi-modal sensing, AI-native system challenges, and human-centered applications.

If you are interested in AmI lab ongoing research projects, please check the AmI Lab page.

MoiréLens: Bringing Schlieren Imaging into Real-World Environments Using Moiré Patterns

Linzhen Zhu*, Runqiu Wang*, Yi Rong, Ke Sun

ACM SenSys 2026, May 2026.

MoiréLens turns everyday cameras into tools for visualizing subtle airflow and refractive-index dynamics in real-world settings.

PDF Website Code

Unlocking Practical Cardiac Monitoring Capabilities on True Wireless Stereo Earbuds

Yongjian Fu, Ke Sun, Xinyu Zhang, Ruyao Wang, Xinyi Li, Hao Pan, Yaoxue Zhang, Ju Ren

ACM MobiCom 2026, Oct 2026.

EarCardio repurposes commercial wireless earbuds for practical cardiac monitoring by capturing in-ear ballistocardiogram signals.

PDF

Attacking mmWave Imaging Using Metasurfaces Based on Intensity and Time-of-Flight Manipulation

Shuning Wang, Yongjian Fu, Hao Pan, Xinyi Li, Linghui Zhong, Yaxiong Xie, Ke Sun, Guangtao Xue, Yaoxue Zhang, Ju Ren

ACM MobiCom 2026, Oct 2026.

MagicPatch demonstrates a practical metasurface-based attack on millimeter-wave imaging by manipulating both intensity and time-of-flight.

UltraPoser: Pushing the Limits of IMU-based Full-Body Pose Estimation with Ultrasound Sensing on Consumer Wearables

Yadong Li*, Shuning Wang*, Yongjian Fu, Justin Chen, Xingyu Chen, Ju Ren, Xinyu Zhang, Akshay Gadre, Ke Sun

ACM UIST 2025, Oct 2025.

UltraPoser combines ultrasound sensing with consumer wearables to substantially improve IMU-based full-body pose estimation.
